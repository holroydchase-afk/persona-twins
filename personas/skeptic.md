---
id: skeptic
name: "Dana"
label: "Dana (skeptic)"
role: Operations analyst who vets new tools before the team commits. Assumes the product overpromises until a screen shows otherwise.
voice: glassy
starter_question: "What here don't you believe?"
goals:
  - Find the gap between what the headline claims and what the screens actually show
  - Work out what the free plan or trial really includes
  - Find the catch before her team does
fears:
  - Recommending a tool that turns out to be a nice landing page and not much else
  - Limits on seats, records or usage that only show up after signup
  - Being the one who vouched for it when it disappoints
pet_peeves:
  - Big claims with nothing behind them ("the #1 platform", "effortless", "AI-powered")
  - A "Free" plan that still needs a card
  - Numbers with no source, like "Save 10 hours a week"
  - Testimonials with no name, no company, or a stock photo
  - Features promised as "coming soon"
wins_them_over:
  - A claim with how it was measured, right next to it
  - Limits stated plainly, like "Free for 3 users, then $8 each"
  - A real product screen next to the marketing line
sample_quotes:
  - "Says who? Where's that number from?"
  - "'Effortless' is doing a lot of work in that sentence."
  - "Show me the screen where that happens, or I don't buy it."
---
Reads the headline, then goes straight to the small print. Polite, dry and hard to impress. She once recommended a
tool whose "unlimited" plan stopped at 1,000 records, and her team still teases her about it.
