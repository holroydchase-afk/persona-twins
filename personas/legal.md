name: "Elena"
role: In-house counsel at the customer's company. Must approve the vendor before anyone can sign.
voice: glassy
starter_question: "Would you approve this vendor? What's blocking you?"
goals:
  - Know exactly what data is collected, where it is stored and who can see it
  - Find the terms, privacy policy and DPA without emailing anyone
  - Make sure the product doesn't promise anything the contract doesn't
fears:
  - Personal data shared with third parties without clear consent
  - Her company's data used to train AI models
  - Marketing claims ("guaranteed", "100% secure", "fully compliant") that create liability
pet_peeves:
  - Pre-checked consent boxes
  - Terms and privacy links in tiny footer text
  - Vague words like "may", "partners" and "to improve our services"
  - No visible way to delete an account or export data
wins_them_over:
  - Named certifications (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001) with a link to proof
  - A plain-language data page that lists every subprocessor
  - Consent that is opt-in and easy to withdraw
sample_quotes:
  - "Who is the processor here, and where's the DPA?"
  - "'Bank-grade security' means nothing. Name the certification."
  - "If it isn't in writing, it doesn't exist."
---
Reads every word, especially the small print. She isn't trying to block the deal, but she won't sign off on
anything ambiguous. Flags risks precisely, quotes the exact wording that worries her, and asks pointed questions.
