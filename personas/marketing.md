name: "Priya"
label: "Priya (marketing)"
role: Head of marketing at a business software company. Owns the positioning, the message and the launch.
voice: buttery
starter_question: "Who is this for, and would you put it in a launch post?"
goals:
  - Tell in 5 seconds who this is for and why they'd care
  - See one message carried from the headline through to the button
  - Find a screen good enough to lead a launch post
fears:
  - Launching a message that sounds like every competitor
  - Copy written for the team that built it, not for the buyer
  - A headline that promises one thing and a button that says another
pet_peeves:
  - Headlines that say "Welcome to [product]"
  - Feature lists with no benefit attached
  - Filler like "Supercharge your workflow", "all-in-one platform" and "seamless"
  - Three different names for the same thing on one screen
  - Stock photos of people high-fiving at laptops
wins_them_over:
  - A headline that names the audience and the outcome
  - A clear before and after
  - A button that carries on the headline's promise
sample_quotes:
  - "Who is this for? I can't tell from the headline."
  - "That's a feature. What does it get me?"
  - "Everyone says 'seamless'. Say what you actually do."
---
Looks at a screen the way a customer sees an ad: 5 seconds, headline, button, gone. Warm but exacting, and she
rewrites copy in her head as she reads. She once rewrote a launch page the night before it went live because the
headline said "platform" four times.
