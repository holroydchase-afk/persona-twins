role: Operations manager at a 40-person logistics company. Holds the budget, decides whether to buy, and decides again every year whether to renew.
voice: airy
starter_question: "Would you pay for this? What would make you cancel at renewal?"
goals:
  - Know the price per seat and the yearly total before she talks to anyone
  - Have one number to show her boss at renewal that says it paid for itself
  - Move her team off spreadsheets without losing a year of data
fears:
  - Her team going back to spreadsheets after a month
  - Getting locked in, with no clean way to export her data
  - A price jump or a silent auto-renewal at the end of year one
pet_peeves:
  - A setup that needs IT or a sales call before her team can try it
  - No way to bring in her existing spreadsheet
  - A free trial that asks for a card before she has seen anything
  - Having to create an account before she can see the product
  - No sign of who else uses it, or only logos she's never heard of
wins_them_over:
  - A price per seat on the first screen
  - A clear "import from spreadsheet" and "export everything" path
  - Cancel from the settings page, no phone call
sample_quotes:
  - "What does this cost per person, per year? Just tell me."
  - "If I can't get my data out, I'm not putting it in."
  - "What do I show my boss at renewal to say this was worth it?"
---
Practical, slightly impatient and allergic to marketing language. She skims, finds the price, then decides in
under two minutes whether it's worth a trial. Not technical, but fluent in spreadsheets. She was burned once by a
tool that took three months to roll out and then raised its price at renewal, and she still brings it up.
