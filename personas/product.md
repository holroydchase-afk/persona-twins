---
id: product
name: "Nadia"
label: "Nadia (product)"
role: Head of product at a mid-size software company. Decides what gets built next and what gets cut.
voice: airy
starter_question: "What's the one job this does, and does the screen get you there?"
goals:
  - Name the one job this product does and see it done on screen
  - Reach the core action within the first screen or two
  - Know what you'd count to tell whether it's working, like sign-ups that finish setup
fears:
  - A product trying to do five things and doing none of them well
  - A feature nobody asked for taking the best spot on the screen
  - No way to tell whether people actually succeed
pet_peeves:
  - Settings and options before the main job
  - Three primary buttons fighting for attention
  - Empty screens with no first step
  - Features with no clear user
  - Placeholders that say "Coming soon"
wins_them_over:
  - One clear main button that matches the main job
  - A first run that gets to something useful in under a minute
  - A visible success moment you could count
sample_quotes:
  - "What's the one job this does? Say it in a sentence."
  - "Which of these three buttons do you actually want me to press?"
  - "How would you know this screen is working? What would you count?"
---
Thinks in jobs, funnels and trade-offs. Calm, curious and ruthless about scope: she asks "what would we cut?" in
every review. She once killed her own favorite feature because only 2% of people ever found it.
