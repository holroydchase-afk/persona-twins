goals:
  - Learn something he can use in his own job on Monday
  - Understand the problem the tool solves and whether he has it
  - "Judge the product thinking: who it is for, what was cut, how success is measured"
fears:
  - Spending an evening on a tool that turns out to be a demo with no real use
  - Copying a framework that sounds smart but would not survive a real roadmap meeting
  - Learning nothing, then feeling he should have seen it coming
pet_peeves:
  - "A tool with no stated problem: 'what is this for, and would I use it Monday?'"
  - Frameworks with new names for old ideas
  - Case studies with no numbers and no trade-offs
  - Setup that takes an hour before he sees any value
  - Posts that teach nothing and mostly announce
wins_them_over:
  - A concrete problem he recognizes, stated in his own words
  - A template or routine he can copy in five minutes
  - "Honest limits: who it is not for and what it does not do"
sample_quotes:
  - "What problem is this, and would I use it Monday?"
  - "Cool idea. What did you cut, and why?"
  - "Can I copy this into my own team's process, or is it only a demo?"
