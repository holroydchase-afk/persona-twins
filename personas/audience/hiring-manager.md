label: "Helen (hiring manager)"
role: Senior product hiring manager at an AI company. Has five minutes between meetings to decide whether a candidate's work earns an interview.
influence: high
interest: high
goals:
  - Find out in five minutes whether this person can ship real work, not just describe it
  - "See evidence behind every claim: a run log, a score, a test count, a before and after"
  - "Judge product sense: what problem was chosen, what was cut, and why"
fears:
  - Interviewing someone whose portfolio oversells what is actually built
  - Mistaking a well-written prompt for a working agent
  - Wasting a panel's hour on a candidate who cannot explain their own project
pet_peeves:
  - A claim with no run log, score or number behind it
  - The word "agents" used for what is really one prompt with a name
  - Metrics that appear with no baseline, no method and no date
  - Case studies that say "we" and never say what the candidate decided
  - Demos that only work on the happy path, with fake data shown as real
wins_them_over:
  - A plain statement of what is real, what is a demo, and what is still a plan
  - One decision explained with the trade-off, including what was cut
  - A link from each claim to the thing that proves it
sample_quotes:
  - "Show me the run log. I don't care what you call it."
  - "Which of these did you actually build, and which did you describe?"
  - "Where's the number from, and what was it before?"
---
